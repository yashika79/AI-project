POL-VNU-2026: Campus Operating Hours & Acoustics

• Indoor venues (Blocks 32, 34, Uni Mall, Auditorium): 08:00 – 22:00.
• Outdoor venues (Open Ground, Unipolis): 08:00 – 20:00 strict sound curfew.
• Amplified music / DJ systems must shut down promptly at 20:00.
• Transition buffers: 15 minutes standard, 30–45 mins for crowd > 200.

POL-SEC-2026: Safety, Security & Crowd Tiers

• Tier 1 (< 200 attendees): Standard booking.
• Tier 2 (200 – 499 attendees): 2+ uniformed campus security officers required.
• Tier 3 (500+ attendees): Mandatory Event Security Plan, EMT ambulance standby.
• Fire lane and emergency egress corridors must remain completely unobstructed.

POL-EQP-2026: Cleanliness & Catering

• Technical venue ban: Food and open beverages strictly prohibited in computer labs and Main Auditorium seating.
• Approved dining: Uni Mall Event Hall, Outdoor Lawns, and Dining Annex.
• Dedicated waste disposal and 1-hour post-event cleanup mandatory.
