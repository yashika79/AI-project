# University Campus Venue & Acoustic Policy (POL-VNU-2026)

## Section 1: Venue Operating Hours and Scheduling Limits
- **Clause 1.1 (Standard Hours)**: All university indoor academic facilities, including seminar rooms, lecture halls, and departmental auditoriums, operate between 08:00 and 22:00 Monday through Saturday. No event may extend past 22:00 without written permission from the Dean of Student Affairs.
- **Clause 1.2 (Outdoor Spaces)**: Outdoor facilities (Amphitheatre, Campus Lawns, Sports Arena grounds) are available from 08:00 to 20:00 only, in order to preserve residential tranquility in adjacent hostel blocks.
- **Clause 1.3 (Transition Buffers)**: Standard indoor bookings must include a mandatory 15-minute transition buffer between consecutive events. For events exceeding 200 attendees or requiring stage setup, a 30-to-45-minute setup and teardown buffer is mandatory.

## Section 2: Campus Noise Control & Acoustic Regulations
- **Clause 2.1 (Academic Quiet Hours)**: Between 08:30 and 17:30 Monday through Friday, sound levels exceeding 65 dB(A) are strictly prohibited in the vicinity of Block A, Block B, and Central Library.
- **Clause 2.2 (Amplified Sound Curfew)**: High-output acoustic systems, live musical bands, and DJ equipment are permitted outdoors only between 16:00 and 20:00 on weekdays, and 10:00 to 20:00 on Saturdays. All amplified music must shut down promptly at 20:00.
- **Clause 2.3 (Auditorium Sound Restrictions)**: The Main Auditorium is equipped with acoustic dampening panels. Sound levels inside the auditorium may reach up to 85 dB(A) provided exterior doors remain sealed during performances.

## Section 3: Cleanliness, Food & Beverage Regulations
- **Clause 3.1 (Food Prohibitions in Technical Venues)**: Consumption of food, snacks, and open beverages is strictly forbidden in Computer Labs, Science Laboratories, and the Main Auditorium seating area. Water in sealed bottles is the sole exception.
- **Clause 3.2 (Approved Catering Venues)**: Catering, dining buffets, and boxed lunches are explicitly permitted in Demo Multipurpose Hall, Executive Conference Dining Annex, and Outdoor Lawn areas.
- **Clause 3.3 (Waste Management & Cleaning Deposit)**: Any event providing catered meals must arrange for dedicated waste disposal bins and submit a refundable cleaning deposit. Event organizers are responsible for restoring venues to clean condition within 1 hour post-event.
