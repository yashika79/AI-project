# University Scheduling Priority, Equipment & Deadlines (POL-OPS-2026)

## Section 1: Scheduling Hierarchy and Conflict Resolution
- **Clause 1.1 (Priority Hierarchy)**: When two or more events contest the same venue and time slot, conflicts are resolved in accordance with institutional priority:
  - Priority Level 1 (Institutional): University Convocations, Official Examinations, and Board of Trustees sessions preempt all other bookings.
  - Priority Level 2 (Academic): Departmental conferences, accredited academic symposia, and scheduled university course lectures.
  - Priority Level 3 (Student Organizations): Recognized student clubs, cultural associations, and sports leagues.
- **Clause 1.2 (Bumping & Rescheduling Protocol)**: If a higher-priority event displaces an existing booking, the Scheduling Office must provide alternative equivalent venue options at least 7 calendar days before the date of the event.

## Section 2: Reservation Deadlines and Cancellation Policy
- **Clause 2.1 (Advance Notice Deadlines)**:
  - Main Auditorium & Large Arenas: Minimum 14 calendar days prior to event.
  - Multipurpose Halls and Lecture Theatres: Minimum 4 calendar days prior to event.
  - Departmental Seminar Rooms: Minimum 48 hours prior to event.
- **Clause 2.2 (Cancellation Deadlines)**: Cancellations must be registered on the University Event Planner portal at least 48 hours prior to the scheduled start time. Failure to cancel without cause results in a 14-day booking suspension for the organizing student club.

## Section 3: Audio-Visual Equipment and Facility Loans
- **Clause 3.1 (Built-in AV Facilities)**: Projectors, smart screens, and fixed microphones are maintained by the IT Helpdesk and included in the venue reservation.
- **Clause 3.2 (Portable Audio & Lighting Kits)**: Wireless hand mics, clip-on lavaliers, and portable PA systems must be reserved at least 3 business days in advance from the IT Media Center and returned within 2 hours of event conclusion.
