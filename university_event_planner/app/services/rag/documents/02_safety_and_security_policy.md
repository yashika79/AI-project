# University Safety, Security & Crowd Management Guidelines (POL-SEC-2026)

## Section 1: Crowd Management and Security Personnel Thresholds
- **Clause 1.1 (Tier 1 Events: Under 200 Attendees)**: Standard event registration with no mandatory campus security guard assignment required, unless monetary admission or external VIP speakers are involved.
- **Clause 1.2 (Tier 2 Events: 200 to 499 Attendees)**: Mandatory deployment of at least two (2) uniformed Campus Security officers for entry verification, fire lane monitoring, and corridor management. Notice must be given at least 5 business days in advance.
- **Clause 1.3 (Tier 3 Events: 500+ Attendees)**: Large-scale gatherings require an approved Event Security Plan, four (4) or more Campus Security officers, emergency exit marshals, and on-site Emergency Medical Technician (EMT) ambulance standby.

## Section 2: Fire Safety, Occupancy Limits, and Emergency Egress
- **Clause 2.1 (Maximum Occupancy Enforcement)**: Under municipal fire codes, venue capacity represents an absolute legal limit. Standing room in aisles, stairs, or exits is strictly prohibited. Violators face immediate event shutdown and disciplinary review.
- **Clause 2.2 (Electrical & Staging Approvals)**: Any external lighting rigs, high-voltage sound amplifiers, pyrotechnics, smoke machines, or stage rigging must be inspected and certified by the University Estates Electrician 24 hours prior to commencement. Open flames and unapproved fog machines are strictly prohibited.
- **Clause 2.3 (Severe Weather Contingencies for Outdoor Venues)**: Organizers of outdoor events (Amphitheatre, Lawns) must register a secondary indoor contingency venue or establish an official cancellation trigger if rainfall or extreme wind exceeds 35 km/h.
